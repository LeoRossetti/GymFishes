### 2.1 The tube as a living aquarium, in stages

> "the plain blue when the tube is full feels kinda shit … some minor bubbles going up, not a lot of bubbles
> and they need to be in bubble patterns which are kinda random, maybe also have some more decoration in the
> tube like water plants, kelp, coral … if the fish you are using is a freshwater fish, the decoration for the
> tube is inspired on rivers and lakes, if it's a saltwater fish, then its inspired by ocean and reefs. It's
> supposed to be in stages, so past a threshold, a decoration is added, all until it's full."

- **Bubbles:** few, in irregular, seeded-random patterns (not the three uniform bubbles M7 shipped and he
  deleted the same day). The celebration already has a seeded bubble field (`src/features/celebrations/
  bubbleField.ts`, `Bubbles.tsx`): reuse the idea, not the density. CSS keyframes on `transform`/`opacity`
  only, paused with the waves (`useWavePause`, `data-waves`), off under reduced motion.
- **Decorations by stage:** one element added per threshold until full (e.g. the five calendar fill steps
  or 25/50/75/100 % of the 3 L scale; decide with him). Two sets, picked by the member's fish habitat:
  freshwater → river/lake (reeds, lily pad, pebbles, driftwood, sand), saltwater → ocean/reef (coral,
  anemone, kelp, shells, rocks). Flat shapes, 1px lines, no gradient/glow; per-species-style palettes are
  the one documented exception to tokens-only colours (spec §8), so a small "decor palette" per habitat
  needs the same explicit exception. Decorations sit behind the water body/at the floor and must never hide
  the fish, the crest or the totals. The tube is 160×220 px (`MemberTube.tsx`, `TUBE_H`).
- **Show first:** render both habitats at every stage (stills) and the bubbles (GIF) on the canvas before
  building anything; Leo judges on his phone.

### 2.3 Achievements are too easy

> "the achievements feel a little too easy like you can unlock about 5 fish in just a day, so they should be
> a bit harder."

- Ladder today (spec §6, `catalog.ts` `UNLOCKS`, `src/features/fish/unlocks.ts`): four starters, then
  bottle / note / before-9h registers (all same-day), streak 3 / 7 / 14, a day above X, N registers, N L
  accumulated. Unlocks are **derived from your own registers, never stored**; `seen_unlocks` (local, per
  device) only decides what has been *celebrated*; "the fish you wear stays yours" is enforced in the
  gallery.
- Making conditions harder will re-lock fish both real users already earned (they have used the app since
  August). Decide with Leo: (a) accept the re-lock on their two phones (leanest; the worn fish stays), or
  (b) persist unlocks (a schema change: an `unlocks` table or a column, RLS, migration; the cloud is the
  only DB). Bring the trade-off to the brainstorm; do not pick silently.
- Direction to propose: at most one same-day unlock; the rest spread over weeks (streaks 5 / 14 / 30,
  registers 50 / 150, volume 30 L / 100 L, a 3 L day, a full week without a miss). Keep the full-screen
  popup as the reward, nothing new.
